A Volleyball Rotation Reference

Off court

Whoever is off court at rotation 1 is the next server, not the last player on your roster — that's what entering here means. Serving order still follows the roster top to bottom.

What's new

  1. v0.53

    • Fixed: shared team links. In some chat apps, tapping the link preview opened an empty VolleyGram instead of the team. If a link you were sent before opened an empty page, ask for it to be sent again.
    • Opening a shared link now says what it added, or that the link was cut short on the way.

  2. v0.52

    • What's new — this page. It's at the bottom of the app, and lights up when there's something you haven't read.
    • “Next in” badge on whoever comes off the bench next rotation, on screen and in saved images, so a screenshot says who's up.

  3. v0.51

    • Sit a player out. Hold them on the court, or press Out on their roster row. They stay on the team but leave the rotation, which works itself out without them. Press Out again to bring them back.
    • New team and Duplicate this team are now at the bottom of the VolleyGram Name list.
    • Replaces v0.50's typed-in subs.

  4. v0.50

    • Temporary subs: hold a player and type in a fill-in. Replaced by sitting out in v0.51.

  5. v0.49

    • Swap players by dragging. Drag one player onto another, or tap two, and they trade places in the lineup — in every rotation. This is now what a drag does.
    • Drag: Move, beside Undo, switches back to positioning players by hand.

  6. v0.48

    • Scoreboard: a game clock that starts on the first point, and the time of day.
    • A −1 on each side instead of one Undo, so a point noticed late comes off the right team.

  7. v0.42 – v0.47

    • The VolleyGram logo, and app icons for your home screen.
    • The players spin in when the app opens. Tap the logo to watch it again.

  8. v0.41

    • The court sizes itself to your screen, and names scale with it.

  9. v0.33 – v0.40

    • Buttons regrouped by what they do. Sharing and saving images live behind Share….
    • Overlap problems are flagged on the players themselves.
    • Off-white sand for beach, with court lines you can see on it.
    • Undo no longer rewinds the scoreboard. The screen stays awake while the scoreboard is up.
    • Players can be moved with the arrow keys.

  10. v0.26 – v0.32

    • Fixed: reordering the roster could snap back when you changed rotation.
    • Fixed: with seven or more, the player off court was drawn off the edge of the screen.
    • Fixed: double-tapping zoomed the page, and the court blocked scrolling.
    • Holding a button no longer selects its text.

  11. v0.23 – v0.25

    • Fixed: with seven or more, the serving order didn't follow the roster. The roster is the serving order.
    • Subs enter at is back, and defaults to zone 1.

  12. v0.22

    • Scoreboard: two teams, big numbers, a record of who won each game.

  13. v0.21

    • Playing surface per team: indoor, grass or beach.

  14. v0.20

    • Short-handed? Five players still rotate through six zones, and the app tells you when nobody's left to serve.

  15. v0.19

    • Optional overlap check: flags players out of rotational order at the serve.

  16. v0.15 – v0.18

    • Share team: the whole diagram travels in a link. No accounts.
    • Save to Photos on phones that support it.
    • Fixed: tapping a player used to pin them in place.
    • The version number in the corner.

  17. v0.13 – v0.14

    • Named VolleyGram.
    • Formation tabs right under the court. The ring now marks whoever is setting.

  18. v0.12

    • Quiz Mode: tap the zone where a player belongs.
    • Images with a transparent background.

  19. v0.11

    • Serve receive and Defense formations, worked out from who's on court.

  20. v0.9 – v0.10

    • Zone numbers on the court. Swipe to change rotation.

  21. v0.5 – v0.8

    • 5-1 and 6-2 systems, plus Simple with no roles.
