A Volleyball Rotation Reference

Off court

Whoever is off court at rotation 1 is the next server, not the last player on your roster — that's what entering here means. Serving order still follows the roster top to bottom.

Add to Home Screen

From your home screen, VolleyGram opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app — and once it's there, it works with no signal.

You're already using VolleyGram from your home screen.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone and the top on an iPad.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

On an iPhone, the home-screen VolleyGram keeps its own teams, separate from the ones in Safari. Add it first, then build your teams there.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install.

Updates arrive by themselves whenever you open it with a signal. Your teams stay on your phone either way.

Referee

When to whistle

Tap one to see who whistles, and what to signal after.

This browser can’t play sound, so the whistles are silent here.

The rules don’t set a length or a number of blasts for any whistle. One whistle stops or starts play, and the hand signal after it says why. If you hear nothing, turn the volume up.

Starting and stopping play

  • Authorisation to serve 1

    Move the hand to indicate the direction of service.

  • Team to serve 2

    Extend the arm to the side of the team that will serve.

  • Time-out 4

    Place the palm of one hand over the fingers of the other, held vertically (forming a T), then indicate the requesting team.

  • Substitution 5

    Circular motion of the forearms around each other.

  • Change of courts 3

    Raise the forearms front and back and twist them around the body.

  • End of set (or match) 9

    Cross the forearms in front of the chest, hands open.

  • Double fault and replay 23

    Raise both thumbs vertically.

In, out, touched

  • Ball “in” 14

    Point the arm and fingers toward the floor.

  • Ball “out” 15

    Raise the forearms vertically, hands open, palms towards the body.

  • Ball touched 24

    Brush the palm of one hand across the fingers of the other, held vertically.

Ball handling

  • Catch 16

    Slowly lift the forearm, palm of the hand facing upwards.

  • Double contact 17

    Raise two fingers, spread open.

  • Four hits 18

    Raise four fingers, spread open.

At the net and the lines

  • Net touched by a player, or a serve into the net that doesn’t cross 19

    Indicate the relevant side of the net with the corresponding hand.

  • Reaching beyond the net 20

    Place a hand above the net, palm facing downwards.

  • Penetration into the opponent’s court, ball under the net, or a foot fault at the serve 22

    Point to the center line or to the relevant line.

Attack, block and position

  • Attack hit fault 21

    Make a downward motion with the forearm, hand open.

  • Blocking fault or screening 12

    Raise both arms vertically, palms forward.

  • Positional or rotational fault 13

    Make a circular motion with the forefinger.

Serving

  • Ball not tossed or released at the service hit 10

    Lift the extended arm, the palm of the hand facing upwards.

  • Delay in service (over 8 seconds) 11

    Raise eight fingers, spread open.

Cards

  • Misconduct warning 6a

    Show a yellow card.

  • Misconduct penalty 6b

    Show a red card.

  • Expulsion 7

    Show both cards together.

  • Disqualification 8

    Show the red and yellow cards separately.

  • Delay warning / delay penalty 25

    Cover the wrist with a yellow card (warning) or a red card (penalty).

Line judges’ flags

  • Ball “in” F1

    Point down with the flag.

  • Ball “out” F2

    Raise the flag vertically.

  • Ball touched F3

    Raise the flag and touch the top with the palm of the free hand.

  • Crossing space fault, ball touched an outside object, or a foot fault at the serve F4

    Wave the flag over the head and point to the antenna or the line.

  • Judgement impossible F5

    Raise and cross both arms and hands in front of the chest.

From the FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028, Diagrams 11 and 12. The numbers are the rulebook’s. School and recreational leagues play by their own rulebooks and may differ.
