VolleyGram — device shelf

The real app, at real widths, side by side. Each frame is live — tap around in it exactly as you would on the device. The frames are built at the listed pixel width and then visually scaled to fit your monitor, so text wrapping and overflow are truthful; only the apparent size changes.

What this is for

  • 320 is the one that bites. Every wrapping bug this app has had showed up there and nowhere else.
  • Check the three-across rows inside Show options — the share row is the tightest, at roughly 80px a column.
  • Open the Scoreboard in landscape, and on a tablet width, to check the score digits. They size off the panel, not the screen.
  • Switch Surface to sand: it is the only one that flips the court lines to dark, so it is the one to check for invisible marks.
  • What this can't show you: touch. Double-tap zoom, scrolling with a finger on the court, and dragging a player are all real hardware questions. A mouse in a frame will not find those.
